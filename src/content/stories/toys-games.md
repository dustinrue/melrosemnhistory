Remember Saturday afternoons on the living room floor? The museum’s toy and game display is a trip back to childhood, with the board games that filled family game nights and the toys that filled the toy box.

Look for Twister, Yahtzee, Parcheesi and Cootie, television favorites like Lassie, The Honeymooners and The Man from U.N.C.L.E., and a Rubik’s Cube or two. Below are a paper big-top circus, toy tractors and a tin barn full of farm animals, a reminder of how many local kids grew up on the farm.

Bring the grandchildren, and see how many you can name.
