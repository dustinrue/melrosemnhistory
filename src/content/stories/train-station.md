All aboard! For generations the railroad was Melrose’s link to the wider world, bringing travelers, mail and freight to town and carrying the area’s goods away.

The museum’s train display brings that era back with the big painted MELROSE station sign, photographs of the rail yards and roundhouse, Great Northern Railway and Burlington Northern keepsakes, and railroad lanterns, keys and a telegraph set. A framed map shows where the roundhouse was planned, and a toy locomotive on a bit of track is a favorite with young visitors.

Have a railroad story, a ticket stub or a family photo from the depot? The Society would love to add it to the collection.
