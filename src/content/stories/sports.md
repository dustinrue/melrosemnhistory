Small towns grow big athletes. The museum’s sports display salutes the Melrose-area players, runners and champions who carried the community’s name into competition, all the way to the Olympic rings.

Behind the glass you will find Team USA gear, athlete photographs and recognition posters honoring women athletes from the region. Come cheer again for the hometown heroes, and tell us who we should add next.

Do you have a trophy, a team photo or a favorite game story? Bring it by, and the Society will help keep it in the record books.
