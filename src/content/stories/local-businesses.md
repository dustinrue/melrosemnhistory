Step back into the days when you knew your baker by name and the grocer delivered to your door. The museum’s business display is a walk down Main Street: the hand-painted Swany White Bakery sign, the Bohlig Cleaners banner, a Herges grocery delivery jacket, and the cash registers and scale that rang up the day’s sales.

Look closer and you will find the everyday things that filled local shelves and kitchens, from syrup and coffee tins to potato chip cans, fruit crates and crockery. Bring someone who remembers, and let the stories start.

Do you have a sign, a ledger or a family photo from a Melrose-area business? The Society would love to hear from you.
