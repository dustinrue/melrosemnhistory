title: "Volunteer with us"
---

Museum volunteers are the heart and soul of the Melrose Area Historical Society’s museum. Each volunteer has a “specialty” area to work in, and all are willing to pitch in on other museum needs. MAHS is proud and thankful to have many dedicated volunteers that have steadfastly taken care of the tasks of maintaining a quality museum and heritage center for the area.

Our volunteers receive tremendous satisfaction from being a part of operating and maintaining the Melrose Area Museum, which has quickly become an asset to this area in Stearns County.

There is always an ongoing need for people of all ages and talents to join this group of volunteers. Some duties include:

- Tour guides (we offer training)
- Organizing displays
- General typing and computer data entry
- Assisting in the research center
- General cleaning and building maintenance
