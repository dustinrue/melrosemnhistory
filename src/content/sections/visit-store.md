The Melrose Area Museum’s store has for sale many publications with information about the history of this area in central Stearns County. Visitors may also choose from several different designs for the History Afghans. These are great for unique gifts for any occasion.

### History Afghans

Afghans with images depicting the historical sites of the Melrose area are available in three colors. These unique afghans will make a very special gift for anyone with an interest in this area.
