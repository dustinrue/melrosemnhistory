---
title: "Visiting the museum"
---

The Melrose Area Museum is open to the public on Wednesdays from 9 to 4, approximately April 15 through September 30. To visit at any other time, call [{{phone}}](tel:{{phone_digits}}) to arrange an appointment — groups and families are welcome.

The building has been renovated for handicapped accessibility. A visit to view these exhibits turns into a learning experience for the whole family, so plan an hour or more.
